The Land That Refuses to Die

Wildgard is no realm of polished tales. It is an ancient valley forgotten beneath the blizzards, where monumental trees cradle the blood of titans and the silence is shattered only by hunter whistles.

Between the black granite crags, the savage horde of the Skorg raised profane totems that choke the weather and cage the wild winds. To reclaim this soil, brute force will not suffice: one must master the whispering undergrowth, the physics of wind-bent arrows, and the sacred embers of solitary campfires.

The Four Tenets of Survival

ARCHERY & ELEMENTAL PHYSICS

The Wind Curves Death

Arrows hand-crafted from harvested resources align with the air currents of WindControl. Bend projectile trajectories to flank heavy shields and strike foes hiding behind stone covers.

TACTICAL MELEE COMBAT

The Weight of Blade & Shield

Relentless and deliberate melee. Precise shield parries break charging momentum, opening gaps for devastating counterstrikes. Fluid evasion and physical momentum govern every swing.

NORSE STEALTH

Shadows in the Mist & Tall Grass

The mist and tall grass are your kin. Stalk unseen across enemy patrol routes, read enemy awareness, and execute lethal takedowns from the shadows without raising the alarm.

CONQUEST & WEATHER

Skorg Totems & Sacred Campfires

Infiltrate enemy outposts and topple runic totems to release weather runes that alter fog and wind across the region. Discovered campfires serve as safe havens and instant fast-travel.
